## 8. Key Messaging Principles

1. **Lead with the outcome, not the technology.** Don't say "AI-powered image generation." Say "photorealistic ad images in 2 minutes."

2. **Anchor on the cost comparison.** $5 vs. $5,000 is the most powerful proof point. Use it early and often.

3. **Acknowledge the limitations honestly.** "AI-generated characters aren't identical across scenes yet — that's why we built Hero Frame Chaining as the workaround. And we're integrating Higgsfield Soul ID for true identity preservation as soon as their API is available."

4. **Position as Creative Director, not a tool.** Canva is a tool. Runway is a tool. Mykos AI is a team member — it does strategy, not just execution.

5. **Show, don't tell.** The demo sells itself. Every sales call should include a live generation.
